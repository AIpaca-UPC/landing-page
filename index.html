How does Rumbo work?

A simple sequence of milestones keeps the trip understandable from start to finish.

01

Pickup

The driver confirms the student's pickup while the vehicle is stopped.

02

Trip

Families consult the current stage and the chronological trip timeline.

03

Delay or incident

An unexpected event can be recorded once and shared with the affected families.

04

Delivery

The delivery is confirmed and becomes part of the trip history.
