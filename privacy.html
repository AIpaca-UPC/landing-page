Privacy Policy

AV1 academic prototype · September 2026

Rumbo is designed around restricted access to information related to school trips and minors. The production product is expected to limit information according to the user's role and authorization relationship.

Data in this Landing Page

This static AV1 version does not create production user accounts. The contact form stores submitted data only in the browser's local storage as prototype evidence and does not send it to a server.

Planned product principles

  • Access to student information should be limited to authorized users.
  • Trip information should be used only for coordination of the school transport service.
  • Users should be able to manage relevant notification preferences.
  • Requests related to personal data should be handled through an explicit process in the production system.
