AV1 academic prototype · September 2026

Rumbo is an academic software project for the UPC Web Applications course. This first version of the Landing Page presents the proposed service and does not represent a production transport operator.

Purpose

The proposed product is intended to support coordination between parents or tutors and school transport drivers through trip statuses, milestones, delays, incidents and notifications.

Prototype limitations

Features shown on this Landing Page may represent planned product behavior. Production accounts, payments, transport contracts and operational guarantees are outside the scope of this static AV1 version.

Acceptable use

Users should provide accurate information, respect authorization boundaries and avoid using the product in ways that could compromise the safety or privacy of minors.

Contact

Questions about this academic prototype can be sent through the contact form on the Landing Page.
